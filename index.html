Privacy Policy.

Your privacy matters. This page explains how FOLD-IT handles information connected to your account and learning experience.

EFFECTIVE DATENovember 18, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to FOLD-IT! Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how FOLD-IT collects, uses, stores, and protects your personal information when you use our application.

FOLD-IT is an educational application designed to help users learn creative napkin folding through step-by-step tutorials, instructional images, and videos.

2. Information We Collect

When you use FOLD-IT, we may collect the following information:

  • Account Information: Your name, email address, and login credentials.
  • Profile Information: Your age, preferred language, and other profile details you choose to provide.
  • Learning Progress: Completed tutorials, saved progress, quiz scores, and learning activities.
  • Usage Information: Basic information about how you interact with the application.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Create and manage your FOLD-IT account.
  • Allow you to sign in securely.
  • Save your tutorial progress.
  • Track completed activities and quiz results.
  • Personalize your learning experience.
  • Improve the application's features and performance.

4. Account Security

FOLD-IT uses authentication services to help protect user accounts. We take reasonable steps to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or misuse.

Users are responsible for keeping their account credentials confidential.

5. Third-Party Services

FOLD-IT may use third-party services to support its functionality, including:

  • Supabase: For authentication, database storage, and account information.
  • Google: If Google Sign-In is enabled, for account authentication.

These services may process information according to their respective privacy policies.

6. Data Storage and Protection

Your account information and learning progress may be stored securely using our application database.

We do not sell your personal information to third parties. We take reasonable measures to maintain the security and confidentiality of stored information.

7. Children's Privacy

FOLD-IT is an educational application. We aim to provide a safe learning environment for students and other users.

We do not knowingly collect unnecessary personal information from children. Where required, parental or guardian consent must be obtained before collecting personal information from children.

8. Your Privacy Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request access to your personal information.
  • Request correction of inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion of your account and associated personal information.
  • Ask questions about how your information is handled.

You may contact us to request assistance with your personal information.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when necessary to reflect changes in our application, services, or privacy practices.

Any updates will be published within the FOLD-IT application.

10. Contact Us

If you have questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact the FOLD-IT development team.
